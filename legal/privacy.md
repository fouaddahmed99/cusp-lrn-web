## Summary
- **No account, no sign-up.** We do not collect your name or email to use {{BRAND}}.
- **Your progress stays on your device.** Answers, flags, notes, flashcard schedules, study plans and settings are stored in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB). We cannot see them.
- **No tracking.** The site does not use advertising or analytics cookies.
- **Backups are yours.** "Export progress" creates a file on your device. We never receive it.

## Hosting
The website is hosted as static files on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses in server logs to deliver and secure the site; see GitHub's privacy statement.

## Error reports and feedback
If you choose to report an error or send feedback, you are taken to a public GitHub Issue form or you email us. Anything you submit there is visible publicly (GitHub) or received by us (email). Please do not include personal or patient information.

## Audio
"Listen" uses your browser's built-in text-to-speech. Voice recordings in OCE practice stay on your device unless you download and share them yourself.

## Children
The service is intended for dental professionals and residents, not children.
