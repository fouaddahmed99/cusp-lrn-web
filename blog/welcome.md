# Welcome to {{BRAND}}

{{BRAND}} is a free study platform for pediatric dentistry residents preparing for their boards.

Our rule is simple: **every fact must be traceable to a free, public primary source.** Questions, notes and flashcards are written only from facts found in those sources, checked automatically against the cited passages, and approved by a person before they appear here. When the sources don't support a topic, we leave a gap instead of filling it in.

There is no account to create. Your progress lives on your device, and you can export a backup at any time from Settings.

If you spot a mistake, use **Report an error** under any explanation. Thank you for helping keep the content accurate.
